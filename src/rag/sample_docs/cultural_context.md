Sri Lankan undergraduate assessment schedules are frequently clustered near
the end of a semester, so several assignments and vivas can fall due within
the same one-to-two week window. Students describe this as more stressful
than the same total workload spread evenly across the term, because there is
little slack time to recover from a single delay.

Strong family expectations around academic achievement, often shaped by the
competitiveness of the Advanced-Level examination system, can add
psychological pressure on top of the raw academic workload itself. Guidance
