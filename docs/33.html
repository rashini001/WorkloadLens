Support through Spectrum.Life

University life can be exciting, challenging, and everything in between. That’s why we’ve partnered with Spectrum.Life a free, confidential wellbeing platform available to all Coventry University students.

Whether you’re looking for mental health support, fitness tips, nutrition advice, or just someone to talk to, Spectrum.Life gives you 24/7 access to expert help and resources, all in one place.

Here’s what you’ll find:

  • Counselling and mental health support in over 35 languages
  • Live chat and phone support available any time, day or night
  • Wellbeing content covering fitness, mindfulness, sleep, and more
  • Personalised tools to help you stay on top of your mental and physical health

You can get free, confidential support at any time through Spectrum.Life. This service is available to all Coventry University students, 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

Access Spectrum.Life

To book counselling sessions or explore wellbeing tools:

  1. Go to the Spectrum.Life website.
  2. Register using the organisation code: CovUni.
  3. Once registered, you can access all wellbeing resources and book sessions.

Tip: If you’d like counselling, calling 0800 0318227 is usually the quickest way to get started.

Ways to get help

Call now to speak to someone:

  • UK Freephone: 0800 0318227
    (Some phone providers may charge for freephone numbers, check with your provider.)
  • Calling from outside the UK: +353 1 518 0277 (standard charges apply)

You can request counselling directly when you call.

Prefer to chat online or request a call back?

  • Go to the Spectrum.Life website to chat online or ask for a call back.
