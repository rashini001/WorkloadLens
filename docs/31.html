Career Guidance and Counselling at NIBM

 

Empowering Students for Career Success

At NIBM | The City University, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. Our Career Guidance and Counselling Unit is dedicated to supporting students in identifying their strengths, exploring opportunities, and making informed decisions about their academic and professional futures.

Through personalized guidance, mentoring, and access to industry insights, we help students navigate their career paths with confidence and clarity.

 

Comprehensive Career Guidance

 

Our career guidance services are designed to connect students with the world of work. We provide:

  • Career Planning Sessions: Helping students align their academic programs with future goals.
  • Workshops & Seminars: Covering employability skills, interview techniques, CV writing, and workplace readiness.
  • Industry Engagement: Exposure to real-world experiences through internships, guest lectures, and industry collaborations.

These initiatives ensure every NIBM student graduates with a clear vision of their career direction and the skills needed to succeed.

Personal Counselling Support

 

Recognizing that personal well-being is key to academic and professional success, NIBM offers confidential Counselling Services to support students’ mental and emotional wellness. Our trained counsellors provide a safe and supportive environment to discuss personal, social, or academic challenges.

By nurturing emotional resilience, we empower our students to overcome obstacles and achieve balance in both their academic and personal lives.

Guidance for Lifelong Growth

 

Career development is a lifelong journey. NIBM’s commitment extends beyond graduation — we continue to provide guidance and networking opportunities through our alumni engagement initiatives and career development programs.

At NIBM, we don’t just prepare students for their first job — we prepare them for a lifetime of success.
